Controls

  • Click a room to select it, shift-click to add it to or remove it from the selection.
  • Drag a room to move the selected rooms. Drag the background to move the map.
  • Shift-drag or right-drag on the background to select rooms inside a rectangle.
  • Mouse wheel zooms in and out.
  • Ctrl-click (or ⌘-click) a room to get directions to it from where you are.
  • Right-click a selected room to delete the selected rooms, right-click an exit to delete it.
  • Double-click an exit to rename it, double-click the background to hide or show the room panel.
  • Red rim is the room you are in, cyan rim is selected. If the room you are in is selected, the selection moves with you.
  • Green rooms are outdoors, grey rooms indoors. Arrows show up/down exits, the oval means some other exit.
